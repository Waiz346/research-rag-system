Title: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive Tasks
Authors: Patrick Lewis, Ethan Perez, Aleksandra Piktus, Fabio Petroni, Vladimir Karpukhin

1. Abstract
Large pre-trained language models store factual knowledge in their parameters, but their ability to access and manipulate precise knowledge is limited. We explore general-purpose fine-tuning recipes for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combining pre-trained parametric and non-parametric memory.

2. Introduction
Parametric memory stores knowledge in neural weights, while non-parametric memory uses a dense vector index of Wikipedia passages.

3. Methodology
We evaluate RAG-Sequence, where the model uses the same retrieved document to generate the complete sequence, and RAG-Token, which can condition tokens on different documents.

4. Experiments
On Natural Questions, WebQuestions, and CuratedTREC, RAG models outperform pure parametric models and generate more factual, specific responses.

5. Conclusion
RAG combines the adaptability of non-parametric knowledge retrieval with the expressive synthesis capabilities of seq2seq generators.
